How it works

Simple. Powerful. Free.

From sensor data to health scores in under a second.

1

Connect sensors

Your existing vibration, temperature, or pressure sensors send data to OpenPMX.

2

Train on your data

OpenPMX learns what "normal" looks like for your specific machines.

3

Get health scores

Every machine gets a real-time score from 0 to 100.

4

Act before failure

Schedule maintenance when it's convenient — not when machines break down.
